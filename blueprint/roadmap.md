# Scale-up roadmap, 12 months

| Phase | Months | Goals | Main deliverables | Exit criteria |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Measure properly | 1 to 3 | Replace assumptions with facts | Timed samples of 30 real tasks per stream; real licence price; first real news feed and usage export in the lake; data curators named | Measured time saved within 30% of self-reported figures, or business case updated with the measured rate |
| 2. Scale the agents | 4 to 6 | Move the two best use cases to all regions | Rapid Response agent and Policy Digest agent in every region; parallel approvals flow live; champions trained in each wave | Low scenario at break-even for each agent; quality rating 4/5 or above; no open review in the portfolio |
| 3. Fix the waiting | 7 to 9 | Cut the remaining delay, which is people waiting | Approval service levels agreed with Legal; sign-off rules for the weekly report; two new use cases from the portfolio moved to pilot | Lead time of each stream at or below target; waiting time down by a third against month 6 |
| 4. Run it as a product | 10 to 12 | Make it part of normal work | Champions network in all regions; quarterly portfolio review with Legal, Privacy and Security; licences assigned by active use; Power BI dashboard owned by the CA AI team | Adoption above 70% of licensed users; ROI base case positive with measured savings |

## Change management in each phase

- Short demos by champions in team meetings, not long trainings.
- A feedback button in every agent answer; feedback reviewed every two weeks.
- Monthly note to leadership with the same six KPIs as the dashboard.
- Clear message on what does not change: people stay responsible for every external statement.

## Risks and how they are handled

| Risk | Handling |
|---|---|
| Time savings overestimated | Count only part of self-reported savings; check with timed samples |
| Low use in late waves | Champions per region; adoption tracked per region every week |
| Wrong or unsourced content in a statement | Sources required in every answer; human approval before anything leaves the team |
| Personal data in prompts or logs | Masking in the silver layer; privacy review before each pilot |
| Licence cost grows faster than use | Licences assigned by active use, reviewed each quarter |
| New regulation, for example the EU AI Act | Each use case screened before pilot; portfolio keeps the risk level and review status |
