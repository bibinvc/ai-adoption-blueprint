# Rapid Response brief

Use this skill when an item lands in the **For Rapid Response** section of the weekly digest, when
a message with the subject "Rapid Response" arrives in the shared mailbox, or when someone asks
for a Rapid Response brief.

## Steps

1. Read the item or the message and note the market, the topic and the deadline if one is given.
2. Search the SharePoint library **Approved positions** for the same topic and market. Use only
   approved documents, never drafts or personal folders.
3. Write a one-page Word brief:
   - What happened, in 3 lines, with the source link.
   - Approved past positions on the topic, each with a link to the document.
   - A draft holding statement of 80 words or less, marked DRAFT.
   - Who must approve: legal liaison, regional head, communications lead.
4. Save the brief in the **Rapid Response** library.
5. Prepare a Teams post for the Rapid Response channel with the brief attached and the three
   approvers mentioned. Ask for approval before posting.

## Rules

- If no approved position exists, say so clearly. Do not invent one.
- No figures, quotes or commitments that are not in an approved document.
- Legal or safety questions: stop and tell the user to contact the on-call legal liaison.
- The draft statement is never published by this skill. The statement owner publishes after approval.
